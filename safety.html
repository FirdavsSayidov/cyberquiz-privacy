English

Cyberkent and its developer "Kiberxavfsizlik markazi" DM have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content, behaviour or use of the Cyberkent app that sexually abuses, exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited.

1. Scope

These standards apply to everyone who uses the Cyberkent app and its related services: visitors, organisers, sellers and staff. They cover every part of the app where a user can enter or show information — account names, profile photos, place of work, wallet transfers and any other content — as well as behaviour towards other people at the event.

2. What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited in Cyberkent:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — any image, video, drawing, text or other material that depicts or describes the sexual abuse or sexual exploitation of a minor (a person under 18), including AI‑generated or altered material;
  • sexualisation of minors in any form, including sexualised profile photos, names or comments involving children;
  • grooming — contacting or building a relationship with a child in order to sexually abuse or exploit them;
  • sextortion — threatening or pressuring a child to obtain sexual images, money or anything else;
  • trafficking of children, or advertising or soliciting children for sexual purposes;
  • sharing, requesting, linking to or promoting any of the above, or helping others to do so.

3. How to report

If you see CSAE content or behaviour in Cyberkent, or believe a child is at risk, report it:

  • by e‑mail to our child safety contact: fsaidov167@gmail.com — please include the account name and a short description; do not attach or forward the illegal material itself;
  • in person to the event organisers at the venue.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local police first (in Uzbekistan dial 102).

4. What we do

  • Every report is reviewed as a priority.
  • Content that breaks these standards is removed, and the account responsible is suspended or permanently closed and its access to the event is revoked.
  • Confirmed CSAM and cases where a child may be in danger are reported to the competent law enforcement authorities of the Republic of Uzbekistan and, where applicable, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or other relevant regional authorities.
  • We preserve the relevant information as required by law and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

5. Compliance with law

"Kiberxavfsizlik markazi" DM complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan on guarantees of the rights of the child and on the protection of children from information harmful to their health, and the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.

6. Child safety point of contact

Cyberkent child safety team, "Kiberxavfsizlik markazi" DM — fsaidov167@gmail.com. This contact is able to speak to our enforcement practices and act on reports.
